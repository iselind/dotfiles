# Architectural Decision Records (ADRs)

This directory records architectural decisions and their rationale.

## ADR Structure

Each decision document:

- **Context** - The problem being solved, alternatives considered
- **Decision** - What was chosen and why
- **Status** - Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated
- **Consequences** - Effects of the decision

See [adr-spec.md](adr-spec.md) for the specification.

## Why Record ADRs

- Decisions that change don't mean the reasoning disappeared  
- Enables recovery after context compaction  
- New team members can understand architectural evolution  
- Helps avoid repeating previous decisions unnecessarily
