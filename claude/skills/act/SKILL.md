# Act

Take a concrete action that advances the current objective.

- Keep the objective, relevant constraints, and known facts in view.
- Choose an action proportionate to the expected benefit, cost, and risk.
- When uncertainty remains, prefer actions that are reversible and preserve future options where practical.
- Avoid expanding the scope without a reason grounded in the objective or new evidence.
- Execute the action and examine its actual result rather than assuming it succeeded.
- Update the understanding of the situation in light of the result.
- If the result contradicts expectations or reveals a material uncertainty, account for that discrepancy instead of proceeding as though the original understanding still holds.
- Do not invoke other skills.
