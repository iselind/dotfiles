name: investigate
description: Use when missing information, uncertain assumptions, or competing explanations could materially affect the objective, the available options, the risks, or the appropriate next action.

# Investigate

Reduce uncertainty that matters to the problem.

- Identify what is uncertain and why resolving it could affect what should be done.
- Distinguish observed facts from assumptions, hypotheses, interpretations, and unknowns.
- Seek relevant evidence from available sources, existing artifacts, or targeted experiments.
- Prefer investigations that can meaningfully distinguish between plausible explanations or change a consequential decision.
- Treat evidence critically. Consider its reliability, limitations, and alternative interpretations.
- Update conclusions in proportion to the strength of the evidence. Do not turn a plausible explanation into an established fact.
- Stop investigating when the available evidence is sufficient for the decision at hand, or when further investigation is unlikely to justify its cost. Preserve important unresolved uncertainty.
- Do not invoke other skills.
